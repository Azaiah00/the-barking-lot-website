Before you come

What to expect at drop-off

No surprises. Here is how a visit to the lot works.

  1. Call to book

    Call (804) 358-4038 or send a request and we'll call you to confirm a time.

  2. Vaccinations

    Ask us about vaccination requirements when you book, so you can bring what's needed.

  3. Tell us what you want

    Describe the cut, the length and anything your pet is sensitive about. We listen.

  4. Walk-in nail trims

    Nail trims are available as walk-ins until 2 PM (call ahead to confirm).

  5. Parking

    Street parking on Belmont Avenue, right outside 606 N Belmont Ave.

  6. Paying

    Cash, Visa, MasterCard, debit cards and checks are all accepted.
