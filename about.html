About the lot

Grooming Richmond since 1972

A neighborhood groomer with old-school standards: Steve Boschen, his shop on North Belmont Avenue, and the pets that keep coming back.

The owner

Meet Steve Boschen

Steve has been grooming pets in Richmond since 1972. That is more than five decades of baths, trims and nail clips for Richmond's dogs and cats.

He is also the photographer behind every portrait on this site. Steve photographs his regulars after their grooms, and those pictures became The Regulars gallery.

The County Advisory Board recognized Steve as a "TOP GUN" business professional.

The shop

A parking lot for dogs

The Barking Lot has served Richmond since 1990. Look closely at the logo: the "i" in Barking is a parking meter.

You'll find the shop at 606 N Belmont Ave, on North Belmont Avenue in the Museum District. It offers professional pet grooming for all breeds, dogs and cats, with street parking right on Belmont.
